# 0.5.5 — Completed replies fold failed attempts into the work record

- After a reply finishes, intermediate tool failures stay in the collapsed processed-work record alongside commands, error details, and retries. The conversation shows the final reply without repeating failed commands in red.
- A manually expanded live work record resets to collapsed when the reply finishes. Pending approvals and questions remain visible.
- Live tool failures and connection/retry feedback remain available. If execution stops without a final explanation, a short warning points to the failed operations in the work record.
